# Riverton Station

Riverton Station opened in 1887 on the Great Western line. It has three platforms;
the third was added in 1962 when freight traffic peaked, and it has been unused
since 1998.

Ridership figures are unreliable before 1974 because the operator counted season
tickets at the originating station only. The 1981 restoration was crucial to the
building's survival — the roof had partially collapsed in 1979 — though the
replacement ironwork is visibly cruder than the original.

The station is unstaffed outside weekday mornings. Local campaigners have argued
for restoring the third platform, but Network Rail's 2019 assessment put the cost
at £4.2m against projected demand it called "marginal."
