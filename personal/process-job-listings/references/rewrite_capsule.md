# Rewriting the Capsule Statement (Headline)

The capsule statement is the professional summary at the beginning of your resume. It is the first thing a recruiter sees when your resume appears in search results. This document explains how to rewrite it for each role based on ATS principles and hiring manager expectations.

## Core Principle

Your capsule statement must include:

0. **Reference my phd in physics, and analytical ability** this is a core strength and I should be refered to as a "physicist" in the headline. This is a strong signal of analytical ability and problem-solving skills, which are highly valued in data-driven roles.
1. **Match the exact job title** from the posting, include looking into transitioning into industry in a [job title] role.
2. **Include 3-4 key skills** drawn directly from the job description
3. **End with a brief value statement** that connects your expertise to business outcomes
4. **Use exact keywords** from the job posting — no synonyms or abbreviations

Having your headline match the exact job title **increases callbacks by 10 times**.

### Example

If the job posting is for "Senior Data Analyst," your capsule could be:

```
Ph.D. in Theoretical Physics with 7+ years of experience applying data-driven methodologies across research and applied environments, seeking to transition into a Senior Data Analyst role. Expertise in statistical analysis, machine learning, and large-scale data processing, with a strong focus on extracting actionable insights from complex datasets. Proven ability to translate advanced quantitative analysis into clear, business-relevant recommendations that drive strategic decision-making. Skilled in Python-based data ecosystems, optimization techniques, and scalable analytics workflows, with a track record of delivering impactful, production-level solutions.
```

Do not write something like:

```
Senior Machine Learning Research Scientist with a Ph.D. in Physics...
```

Instead, write something like:

```
Ph.D. Physicist looking to transition into a Senior Machine Learning Research Scientist role. 
```

## Step-by-Step Rewrite Process

### 1. Extract the Exact Job Title

- Copy the job title directly from the posting. Do not paraphrase or "improve" it.
- If the posting says "Senior Project Manager," use exactly that.
- If it says "Staff Engineer – Infrastructure," use exactly that (including hyphens and capitalization).

### 2. Identify 3-4 Primary Technical Skills

- Scan the job description for the most frequently mentioned hard skills.
- Prioritize skills that appear in the "Required Skills" or "Must Haves" section.
- Focus on tools, technologies, and methodologies — not soft skills like "leadership" or "communication."
- Examples of valid hard skills: SQL, Python, Tableau, Power BI, ETL, Salesforce, Kubernetes, AWS, React, Figma.

### 3. Extract Keywords from the Job Description

- Copy key phrases directly from the posting. Do not translate or use synonyms.
- If they say "data storytelling," write "data storytelling" (not "data visualization").
- If they say "stakeholder communication," write "stakeholder communication."
- If they say "cross-functional collaboration," write "cross-functional collaboration."

### 4. Craft a Brief Value Statement

- Keep it to 5-10 words maximum.
- Connect your skills to a business outcome (revenue, efficiency, insights, growth, etc.).
- Avoid vague phrases like "dedicated professional" or "results-driven."
- Examples:
  - "Turning data into insights that drive revenue"
  - "Automating workflows to save time and reduce costs"
  - "Building scalable infrastructure that powers growth"
  - "Creating user experiences that increase engagement"

### 5. Assemble the Capsule Statement

## Hard Constraints

- **No fabrication.** All skills must be truthfully present in your professional background or resume. Never add skills you don't have.
- **No soft skills in the capsule.** Terms like "leadership," "communication," "teamwork," or "problem-solving" do not belong in a headline. Save those for bullet points.
- **Exact word matching.** If the job says "machine learning," write "machine learning" — not "AI" or "deep learning."
- **No abbreviations unless universal.** ATS systems do not understand "LA" as "Los Angeles" or "ETL" as optional. Use full terms or only abbreviations that are industry-standard and appear in the posting.

## Validation Checklist

Before finalizing the capsule statement, verify:

- [ ] Does the job title exactly match the posting (case and hyphens included)?
- [ ] Are all 3-4 skills hard skills (tools, technologies, frameworks, methods)?
- [ ] Are all skills truthfully represented in your background?
- [ ] Is there at least one keyword phrase copied directly from the job description?
- [ ] Is the value statement concrete and connected to a business outcome?
- [ ] Does the entire statement fit on one line (roughly 80-100 characters)?
- [ ] Can all the text be selected and copied without special characters or corruption?

## Common Pitfalls

| Mistake | Example | Fix |
|---------|---------|-----|
| Changing the job title | Posting says "Senior PM", you write "Project Manager" | Use the exact title as stated |
| Using synonyms | Posting says "data pipeline", you write "ETL" | Copy keywords word-for-word |
| Including soft skills | "Senior Analyst — Leadership \| Communication \| Excel" | Remove soft skills, add technical tools |
| Vague value statement | "Driven professional with a passion for analytics" | "Delivering insights that accelerate growth" |
| Adding skills you don't have | Posting requires Python, you add it even though you don't use it | Only include skills from your actual background |
| Over-complicated capsule | More than 4 skills or a sentence longer than one line | Keep it tight: title, 3-4 skills, value statement |

## Testing Your Capsule

After writing the capsule statement, ask:

1. **Would a recruiter searching for "[Job Title]" find this?** ✓ If yes, your title matches. You can use a tool like `grep` to check for exact matches in the job description.
2. **Are the keywords in the capsule also in the job description?** ✓ If yes, you're using their language. You can use a tool like `grep` to verify this.
3. **Can I back up every skill claim with a project or role on my resume, or an acceptable skill addition?** ✓ If yes, you're being truthful.
4. **Does the capsule sound like me or like an AI wrote it?** ✓ If it sounds like you, keep it. Otherwise, simplify. This will be checked during the ai writing check later.

---

**Remember:** The capsule statement is not marketing copy — it is a search index entry. Make it scannable, factual, and rich in the keywords that ATS systems and humans are looking for.
